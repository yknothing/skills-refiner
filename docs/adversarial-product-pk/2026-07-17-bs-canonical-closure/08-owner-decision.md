# Final Product Judgment

**Decision:** accept-with-limitations for the requested scope (P0, P1, P2-9, P2-10).

The machine now has a coherent Better-Skills namespace story: `bs-*` only on discovery and authoring for the twin family, governance prefers `skill-hygiene`, SDLC prefers `prodcraft`, scans can finish without hanging on giant trees.

Limitations are intentional: policy is social/process enforcement, not an installer lock; provenance skip/cap trades completeness for completability; `bs-skill-health` remains a naming footgun mitigated by docs only.

**Promotion boundary:** machine-local policy docs and quarantine shelves are canonical for this host. Do not promote “organization-wide skill standard” without L3/L4 review and installer integration.
