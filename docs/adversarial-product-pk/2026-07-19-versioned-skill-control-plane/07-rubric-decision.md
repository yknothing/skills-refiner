Scoring follows the `adversarial-product-pk` 0–3 rubric. Acceptance certifies this architecture package, not implementation, migration, market demand or runtime savings.

| Dimension | Score | Reason |
|---|---:|---|

## Threshold result

- No dimension scores 0.
- More than eight dimensions score 2 or 3.
- All critical/high objections are resolved at the architecture level through scope removal, a selected design, a hard gate or an explicit limitation.
- No critical objection is hidden; all corresponding implementation/migration vetoes remain visible.

**Decision: Accept with limitations.**
