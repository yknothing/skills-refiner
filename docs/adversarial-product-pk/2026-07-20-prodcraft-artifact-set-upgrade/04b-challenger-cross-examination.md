# Challenger Cross-Examination

## Cross-examination posture

- Role: Challenger, second round.
- Review level: remains L2 agent-separated. Reading the Champion first pass is intentional cross-examination, not a new independent first pass.
- Visible in this round: ADR-0004, shared evidence packet, `02-champion-first-pass.md`, and my `03-challenger-first-pass.md`.
- Promotion rule applied: an amendment request, invariant, validation plan or future hard stop is not itself an allowed resolution. Resolution requires new evidence, removed scope, a test/gate that exists and is runnable, an explicit Owner decision, accepted unresolved risk, or rejection with evidence.

## Bottom line

The Champion has identified several real defects and proposed useful amendments, but **none of the eight amendments currently resolves O-01..O-12**. They are requests inside a role artifact, not changes to the target ADR, runnable gates, new evidence or Owner decisions.

At the canonical-architecture boundary:

- O-11 can be contained by **scope removal** if the ADR continues to withhold all context-saving claims and binds future evidence per Agent/profile.
- O-06 and O-08 can move from veto pressure to **explicit Owner risk acceptance** because transparent external-writer interception and continuous runtime freshness are deliberate non-goals.
- The remaining critical objections require actual architecture changes or evidence. They cannot be converted into “accepted limitations” while the contradicted claims remain in the ADR.

Therefore my current recommendation remains: **veto canonical promotion**. The Champion's `accept-with-limitations` recommendation becomes supportable only after the minimum解除清单 below is incorporated and recorded through allowed resolution types.

### Finding on the amendments

The amendments are valuable because they prevent premature acceptance, but their current resolution label is not “resolved.” The correct label for all eight is **proposed resolution path**. A4 and A8 narrow ambiguity/scope; neither supplies the missing evidence. Calling them resolved would be the exact gate-as-proof false-consensus failure identified in the Challenger first pass.

### Invariant verdict

The invariants mostly describe a sound target state. They are not evidence that the target can be reached or maintained. Two are materially misleading unless narrowed:

1. I7 cannot simultaneously mean one global profile and Agent-specific exposure; the identity cardinality must be decided.
2. I8/I11 cannot imply continuous effective-surface authority when Agents and raw installers can read/write outside controller command boundaries.

## Minimum解除清单 for canonical promotion

This list is deliberately smaller than implementation acceptance, but every item must appear in the target architecture or an authoritative referenced contract. Merely repeating a fitness function is insufficient.

1. **Resolve the locator authority conflict.** Define a controller-owned projection envelope separately from upstream `prodcraft-runtime-locator.v1`; name the verifier and its execution boundary. Narrow fail-closed claims to what that verifier actually mediates. Provide at least a runnable fixture validator for wrong repository, generation, digest and path escape.
2. **Narrow gateway-only availability claims.** State that `pc-prodcraft` is the only metadata-discoverable Skill in this profile. Treat downstream routed usability as Agent-specific qualification, not an automatic consequence of storing the repo. An unqualified Agent must use a named fallback or remain outside the deployment claim.
3. **Define state predicates instead of one “installed” label.** At minimum: stored, qualified, projected, discoverable, routable, loaded/observed. Store-only state must not produce installed/READY authority.
4. **Resolve profile cardinality.** Make profile selection per Agent/root with one artifact generation, or restrict V1 to one Agent/profile. Define how status, apply and undo represent heterogeneous profiles without calling them a mixed generation.
5. **Close the 46→40 decision surface.** Require a complete old-item disposition and root/collision inventory. The seven retirements require explicit Owner approval; same-basename mapping alone is not semantic replacement evidence.
6. **Define the transaction property honestly.** Specify durable publish/recovery ordering and explicitly state that multi-path visibility is not atomic unless an enforceable barrier exists. “Transactional” may mean approved immutable plan plus crash-consistent exact pre/post/`RECOVERY_REQUIRED`, not invisible batch rename.
7. **Make recovery material P0 and independent.** Define retained bytes and reachability for adopted pre-state, active artifact and prior generation outside the single collection-subtree loss domain. Define the exact rollback identity schema. If whole-control-root loss is outside the guarantee, remove the contrary repair/undo claim.
8. **Stage V1 before live mutation.** Canonical architecture may authorize only: read-only resolve/qualify/plan → sandbox single-root generation/undo → one real-Agent profile qualification → multi-root live migration. Each transition needs a named hard stop; no live 46→40 operation is part of the first proof step.
9. **Record the two operational exposure contracts.** Raw external installers remain competing filesystem writers, and health is command-time rather than continuous. These must be explicit Owner decisions or removed through stronger enforcement.
10. **Preserve the promotion ceiling.** Architecture acceptance authorizes an implementation plan only. Mutation, context savings, Agent availability, upstream stability and exact rollback remain separately gated claims.

If items 1–8 are incorporated through scope removal, new architecture evidence or runnable gates, and items 9–10 receive the required decisions, I would withdraw the canonical-architecture veto while retaining the implementation/mutation veto.

## Risks requiring explicit Owner acceptance

These risks are legitimate value choices after the structural contradictions above are removed. They must not be silently converted into engineering facts.

### R-Owner-01 — Unsupported raw installer exposure window

- **Risk:** `npx skills add/update/remove` may replace or delete an Agent-facing projection before the next managed reconcile.
- **Required decision:** accept that raw global installer commands are unsupported while ProdCraft is managed, that they can cause immediate effective-surface drift, and that detection/repair is command-time rather than preventive.
- **Must not be claimed:** raw installer activity cannot change what an Agent sees.
- **Related objections:** O-06.

### R-Owner-02 — Command-time, not continuous, health

- **Risk:** a `READY` result is valid only for its target roots, Agent evidence and observation instant/expiry; filesystem reality can change afterward.
- **Required decision:** accept the mutation-to-reconcile detection window because V1 has no correctness-dependent watcher or loader hook.
- **Must not be claimed:** the controller continuously guarantees runtime health.
- **Related objections:** O-08.

### R-Owner-03 — Attestational quiescence and unknown consumers

- **Risk:** if the new-session barrier is Owner-attested rather than technically enforced, an unknown or accidentally started consumer may observe transient mixed paths during multi-step cutover.
- **Required decision:** identify who may attest quiescence, which known consumers are covered, and whether any unknown consumer blocks the operation. Accept residual exposure only after mutation-root completeness is proven.
- **Must not be claimed:** the migration provides atomic visibility to all machine processes.
- **Related objections:** O-05, A2, A3.

### R-Owner-04 — Per-Agent fallback tradeoff

- **Risk:** some Agents may fail gateway-only qualification and need full compatibility or exclusion, losing context containment or collection availability for those roots.
- **Required decision:** approve the per-Agent/profile matrix and accept that no machine-wide default benefit is claimed until each participating adapter is qualified.
- **Must not be claimed:** one profile gives both minimal context and full usability to every Agent.
- **Related objections:** O-02, O-10, O-11.

### R-Owner-05 — Legacy capability retirement

- **Risk:** the seven legacy-only names may represent intentionally lost capabilities rather than redundant package names.
- **Required decision:** approve each retirement in the 46-row disposition ledger after reviewing successor/absence evidence.
- **Must not be claimed:** 46→40 is behavior-preserving solely because the arithmetic balances.
- **Related objections:** O-04.

### R-Owner-06 — Receipt remains stale and operationally confusing

- **Risk:** `.skill-lock.json` remains external history that may disagree with the managed deployment and influence future raw CLI behavior or operator assumptions.
- **Required decision:** accept explicit receipt drift in V1 and the requirement to use managed status as deployment authority.
- **Must not be claimed:** third-party installer history is synchronized with the active generation.
- **Related objections:** O-06.

## Risks that Owner acceptance alone must not waive

The following are claim contradictions, not ordinary residual risks. They require scope removal or evidence before canonical promotion:

- O-01: claiming runtime generation/digest fail-closed behavior without an enforcing component.
- O-03: calling store-only content installed/READY.
- O-05: calling a multi-path recoverable sequence atomically visible.
- O-07/O-09: claiming exact repair/undo without surviving bytes and a complete identity contract.
- O-10: claiming Agent-specific exposure while generation/profile identity remains singular and undefined.

An Owner may choose a weaker product contract, but that choice must remove the stronger claim; “accepted unresolved” cannot leave mutually incompatible architecture invariants in canonical text.

## Final Challenger answer

- **Canonical promotion now:** veto.
- **Champion amendments:** substantive diagnosis, but currently proposed paper gates; none is formally resolved.
- **Potentially contained without implementation proof:** O-11 through explicit scope removal; O-12 only after staged V1 scope is made canonical.
- **Owner decisions still required:** raw-installer exposure, command-time freshness, attestational quiescence, per-Agent fallback, seven retirements and stale receipt UX.
- **Implementation/mutation authority after canonical promotion:** remains vetoed until exact-artifact runnable gates and real-Agent/fault/undo evidence pass.
